Facilities

What we offer, in plain language

Each facility has its own staff, instruments and way of working. Pick the one that matches your question, then follow its page for the details.

  • Microscopy CSI · LIGHT, ELECTRON AND X-RAY

    See the structure of your sample, from whole organs and seeds down to organelles and nanometre-scale surfaces. Widefield and confocal fluorescence, STED super-resolution, light sheet, micro-CT and scanning electron microscopes, with image analysis support and training for every user.

    fluorescence imaging3D imagingmicro-CTSEMimage analysis
    CSI Microscopy siteMicroscopy basics for new usersBook an instrument

  • Plasma Spectrochemistry ICP-OES · ICP-MS · ELEMENTAL ANALYSIS

    Find out which elements your sample contains and how much of each: metals and most non-metals, from major components down to parts per trillion. Soil, water, plant material, food, biological tissue, metals, polymers and pharmaceuticals, with sample digestion done in the lab. Samples are submitted with a form; no booking needed.

    trace elementsheavy metalsnutrients in soil and plantswater qualityalloy impurities
    ICP lab pageSubmit samplesFAQ

  • Mass Spectrometry MOLECULAR ANALYSIS

    Identify and quantify molecules, from small metabolites to proteins, by their mass. Page in preparation; contact us for current capabilities.

  • Bio-Materials Analysis and Testing MATERIALS CHARACTERISATION

    Physical and chemical characterisation of biological and bio-based materials for research and industry. Page in preparation; contact us for current capabilities.
